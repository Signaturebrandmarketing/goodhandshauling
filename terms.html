Quotes

Send us a photo and we will give you a price, usually within about 15 minutes during business hours. A photo quote is an estimate based on what we can see. If the actual job is materially larger than the photo showed — more volume, heavier material, longer carry, or items that require special handling — we will tell you the revised price before we load anything. No surprises. We do not add charges after a job is complete. The price you accept is the price you pay.

Scheduling

Same-day service depends on truck availability. If we cannot make a window we have promised, we will call you — we would rather move you than leave you waiting. Please give us as much notice as you can if you need to cancel or reschedule.

What we haul

Furniture, appliances, household junk, garage and shed contents, yard and storm debris, renovation and construction debris, sheds, hot tubs, playsets, and whole-house or estate cleanouts.

What we cannot take

By law and by disposal-site rules, we cannot load or transport:

  • Paint, solvents, chemicals and other liquids
  • Hazardous or flammable materials
  • Asbestos or suspected asbestos-containing material
  • Medical or biological waste

Polk County's household hazardous waste facility accepts these items from residents at no charge, and we will tell you exactly where to take them. Certain items carry a disposal surcharge, which we will always quote up front: refrigerators and air conditioners (refrigerant), mattresses, tires, and older tube televisions.

Access and property

You confirm that you own the items being removed, or have permission to have them removed. Please make sure we can reach the items and park nearby. We protect doorways and floors and sweep the space before we leave. If something is damaged because of our work, tell us right away and we will make it right.

Items are not recoverable

Once items leave your property they go to a disposal site or to donation and cannot be retrieved. Please check for anything valuable, sentimental or important before we load — we will always ask, but the final look is yours.

Donation
